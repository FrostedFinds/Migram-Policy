Migram Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Migram ("we," "our," or "the app") is a personal migraine tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Migram collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Migram collects only the information necessary to provide its tracking and insights features:

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Migraine attack entries: data you choose to log, including pain level, duration, location, symptoms, triggers, and medications taken.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Migram Pro subscription, managed through our payment processing partner.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what you directly enter into the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To store and display your migraine history back to you.
  • To calculate the trigger and symptom pattern insights shown in the app.
  • To generate PDF reports you choose to create and share, at your request.
  • To manage your account login and subscription status.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Migram uses the following service providers to operate:

  • Supabase — securely stores your account and migraine entry data.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and status; processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play, depending on your device.

These providers process data only as necessary to provide their services to Migram and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to your migraine entries is restricted to your own authenticated account.

Your Choices

  • You may edit or delete individual migraine entries at any time within the app.
  • You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us using the information below.
  • You may cancel your Migram Pro subscription at any time through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

Children's Privacy

Migram is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
